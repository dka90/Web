Flagship Product

RMS Excel

RMS Excel is our Risk Management System built directly on top of Excel. It streams live market data into your spreadsheets and layers on real-time risk and exposure tracking — so you get a purpose-built risk tool without leaving the environment your team already relies on.

  • Live price feed integrationStreams real-time market data directly into Excel cells.
  • Position & exposure trackingSee live exposure across instruments, desks, or accounts.
  • Custom risk alertsSet thresholds and get notified the moment a limit is breached.
  • Configurable dashboardsLayouts tailored to how your desk actually reviews risk.
  • No workflow disruptionRuns inside Excel — no new platform for your team to learn.
