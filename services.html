Excel Add-in

LiveGrid RTD — real-time NSE, BSE & MCX prices, inside Excel

LiveGrid RTD streams live quotes from every major Indian exchange and segment straight into Excel cells with =RTD() formulas — over a plain internet connection, with no manual refresh and no stale data waiting on F9.

  • Drop-in RTD formulasExisting =RTD("rtdserver",,"symbol","field") formulas just work — no macros or rebuilding sheets.
  • Sub-second, tick-true updatesCells move with the market, not with a refresh button.
  • Multi-exchange, one feedEquities, futures, options, currency, and commodity quotes across NSE, BSE, MCX, and NSE Com.
  • Resilient by designA VBA fallback timer keeps data flowing even if the native callback path is blocked.
