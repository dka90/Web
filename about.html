About XQG Group

Where markets and technology meet.

XQG Group exists to close the gap between financial markets and the technology that supports them — building tools like RMS and LiveGrid RTD for teams that need better visibility into their risk and markets, and taking on freelance projects for those who need focused market and data expertise.

Our Mission

Make market technology accessible, not just powerful.

A lot of financial technology is built for large institutions with large budgets. We build and freelance with a different starting point: solutions and analysis that fit inside the tools and budgets that traders, desks, and smaller firms already have — starting with Excel, and going anywhere from there.

Markets First

Every tool starts from how markets and trading actually work.

Built to Fit

Solutions shaped around your workflow, not the other way around.

Risk-Aware

Risk visibility is treated as a first-class feature, not an afterthought.

Data-Backed

Clean, reliable data underpins every product and every analysis.

Practicality
